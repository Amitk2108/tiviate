# API keys for Movies & Series (optional)

Movies & Series work without any keys: Tiviate shows what your provider sends (posters, descriptions,
backdrops, trailers, episodes). With your own **free** keys, it adds more:

| Service | What it adds | Free limit |
|---|---|---|
| **TMDB** (themoviedb.org) | Descriptions and pictures when the provider has none, the cast with photos and their pages, **Top 10 today**, **Because you watched…** | Generous for personal use |
| **OMDb** (omdbapi.com) | **IMDb** ratings and **Rotten Tomatoes** scores on a title's page (needs TMDB too) | 1,000 lookups a day |
| **OpenSubtitles** (opensubtitles.com) | **Subtitles → Search online…** in the film player | A few downloads a day |

Tiviate ships no keys: each person uses their own, so nobody runs into someone else's limits.

## Where to enter them

*Settings → API keys*. Type each key once, or use **Easier with your phone** on the same page: scan the QR code
with your phone, paste the keys there and send them. Then press **Save and check**: each key shows
*Key works*, *Key not accepted* or *No key*.

## Getting the keys

Use a phone or a computer for the websites.

### TMDB
1. Create a free account on [themoviedb.org](https://www.themoviedb.org/signup).
2. In your account settings, open **API** and ask for an API key (personal use). Any short description is fine.
3. Copy the **API Key** (the short one, *not* the long "Read Access Token") into *TMDB*.

### OMDb
1. Open [omdbapi.com/apikey.aspx](https://www.omdbapi.com/apikey.aspx), choose **FREE** (1,000 lookups a day)
   and enter your email.
2. Open the link in the email to turn the key on, then copy the key into *OMDb*.

### OpenSubtitles
1. Create a free account on [opensubtitles.com](https://www.opensubtitles.com/).
2. Open [opensubtitles.com/consumers](https://www.opensubtitles.com/consumers), add a consumer (any name) and copy
   its **API key** into *OpenSubtitles*.
3. A free key allows a few subtitle downloads a day; the player says when today's are used up.

## Privacy

To look titles up, Tiviate sends their names (and the IMDb id of the title you open) to these services, with your
key. Your playlist, provider logins and viewing history stay on the TV. Without a key, Tiviate sends nothing to
that service. Answers are kept on the TV for a while, so the same title isn't looked up again and again.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
